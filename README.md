# Vanta Reserve

Vanta Oil – Mobile-First Investment Website Prompt

Create a premium, production-ready oil investment website called Vanta Oil. The design should feel like a modern fintech platform with luxury energy branding. The entire experience must be mobile-first, responsive, smooth, and optimized for phones before tablets and desktops.

Design Language

Premium, elegant, and trustworthy.

Static, clean design (avoid overly flashy animations).

Primary color: Muted Oil Yellow (#C9A227 or similar).

Secondary colors: White, Charcoal Black (#1A1A1A), Soft Gray (#F5F5F5).

Avoid bright neon yellows.

Rounded corners (16–24px).

Premium typography with excellent spacing.

Soft shadows and subtle gradients only.

Apple-inspired UI with a professional fintech feel.

Overall Goal

The final product should feel like a premium oil investment platform rather than a generic template. It should inspire trust, look luxurious, and provide a smooth mobile experience. The muted yellow theme should reflect energy, wealth, and professionalism without appearing overly bright or distracting. The authentication pages should be unique, and the global Vercel-style shimmer circular loading spinner should create a polished experience throughout the entire website.
